Exiting persistence keeps elements mounted and plays their exit animation before they are removed
from the DOM. Without it, elements are removed immediately and no exit animation plays.

It remains available for existing integrations while its remaining use cases are validated. Do not
introduce it in new [Motion](/components/motion/motion-primitive) implementations; keep Motion
mounted and control its lifecycle with `action="enter"` or `action="exit"` instead.

The examples below document existing ExitingPersistence behavior, including integrations with
[legacy entering-motion](/components/motion/entering-motion/examples) components.

Set the `appear` prop to also trigger the entering animation when the component first mounts (not
just on subsequent renders).
